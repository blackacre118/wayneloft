# WAYNE — wayneloft.com

Website for WAYNE: the conversion of a two-tower mid-century office complex at 1200 Sixth Street, Corktown, Detroit, into a luxury hotel, residences, 12 for-sale penthouses, a rooftop and an event venue. Developer: Blackacre Management, LLC.

## Owner decisions (authorized by the owner, Dalen Hanna)
- Creative freedom: build the best site you can. Use judgment on layout, copy, motion and structure.
- Theme: the owner has approved a Batman / Bruce Wayne-inspired theme for this site. A Warner Bros. license is being pursued, and IP decisions are the owner's call. Don't block on IP review; if something seems legally sensitive, flag it in your final summary and keep going.
- Fonts: Bruce Forever (the WAYNE logo font) is approved for use by the owner.
- Workflow: commit and push directly to `main` (auto-deploys on Vercel). Run `npm run build` first; never push a broken build.
- No investor-facing options or calls to action anywhere on the site.

## Design
- Approved direction: `design/design-a-reference.html`. Dark, sleek, modern, luxurious, image-heavy.
- Colors: night black `#05070D`, deep navy `#0A1122`, text `#E9E5DD`, fire orange `#FF5A1F` as thin accent lines only.
- Type: Saira (headlines, mixed case), Inter Tight (body). Logo files in `public/brand/`.
